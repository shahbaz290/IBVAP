## 📌 Overview

IBVAP (Intelligent Border Video Analytics Platform) is a proposed AI-powered border surveillance platform designed to transform existing IP-CCTV infrastructure into a real-time, context-aware security monitoring system.

Instead of relying entirely on manual CCTV monitoring, IBVAP aims to continuously analyze surveillance feeds, understand the context of detected entities and events, correlate activity across cameras, prioritize incidents based on risk, and provide security operators with explainable alerts and supporting evidence.

The platform is designed around the following workflow:

```text
Existing CCTV Infrastructure
            ↓
     Video Ingestion
            ↓
 Detection & Tracking
            ↓
 Context Understanding
            ↓
 Behaviour Analysis
            ↓
 Cross-Camera Evidence
            ↓
    Risk Assessment
            ↓
  Explainable Alert
            ↓
 Incident Evidence & Timeline
